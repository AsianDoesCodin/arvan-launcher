Wizard

Spellcaster who grows with Intelligence. At level 15 you pick one of three routes of magic. Every Wizard shares the skills below.

Base stats

INT 10

Stat growth per level

DEX x0.5VIT x0.5STR x0.5

Starter kit

Apprentice Focus, Potion of Healing x5, 10 bread, Bamboo Crane Seal Ring

The three paths

Flame Route

Fire magic · 8 skills

Fire magic: bolts, barrages, breath and burning dashes.

Ice Route

Frost magic · 6 skills

Frost magic: icicles, frost waves, ray of frost and a protective ice block.

Priest Route

Healing and support · 9 skills

Holy healing, support, and radiant magic
