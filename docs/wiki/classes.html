Classes

Arvan has two classes, Warrior and Wizard. At level 15 each splits into three paths, so there are six ways to play. Every skill has ranks you upgrade with Spirit and Skill Upgrade Books.

Warrior 3 paths

Duelist

Speed and evasion · 18 skills

Dexterity speed path. Uses swords, daggers, katanas, and fists.

Reaver

Raw damage · 17 skills

Strength damage path. Uses longswords, dual swords, and twinblades.

Paladin

Defence and holy power · 14 skills

Defensive Vitality path. Uses axes, greatswords, and hammers.

Wizard 3 paths

Flame Route

Fire magic · 8 skills

Fire magic: bolts, barrages, breath and burning dashes.

Ice Route

Frost magic · 6 skills

Frost magic: icicles, frost waves, ray of frost and a protective ice block.

Priest Route

Healing and support · 9 skills

Holy healing, support, and radiant magic
