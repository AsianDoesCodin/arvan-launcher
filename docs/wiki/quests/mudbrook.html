Mudbrook quests

Lv 60-80. A red-roofed jungle stilt village beside the Mosscrag mine. 20 quests.

The Road to Mudbrook #118

Elder Mirela has asked you to carry word to Mudbrook, the jungle village beside the Mosscrag mine, on the island about 350 blocks south-west of Reedwater. Find Foreman Quill in the stilt hall. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Foreman Quill
Mirela sent you? Good; we need every arm we can get. Three weeks ago my miners up at the Mosscrag broke into a cave nobody knew was there, an old smugglers' den. Its crew never left. They're dead, and they still guard their gold, and everything in the bogs has gone mad with them.

Bog Toads #119

Defeat 8 Bog Toads in the island's jungle south of Mudbrook, then return to Foreman Quill in the stilt hall in Mudbrook.

Goal Defeat 8 Bog ToadTurn in to Foreman Quill
That's 8 fewer toads. The bog path is open again.

Drowned Corsairs #120

Defeat 8 Drowned Corsairs in the island's jungle north-west of Mudbrook, then return to Forager Nell in the west stilt hall in Mudbrook.

Goal Defeat 8 Drowned CorsairTurn in to Forager Nell
Those corsairs drowned once already. Thanks for making it stick.

Mire Crocodiles #121

Defeat 8 Mire Crocodiles in the island's jungle south-east of Mudbrook, then return to Forager Nell in the west stilt hall in Mudbrook.

Goal Defeat 8 Mire CrocodileTurn in to Forager Nell
Good. The crocodiles won't take any more of my miners.

Moss Golems #122

Defeat 3 Moss Golems in the island's jungle north-east of Mudbrook, then return to Foreman Quill in the stilt hall in Mudbrook.

Goal Defeat 3 Moss GolemTurn in to Foreman Quill
3 Moss Golems! The crag shook every time one fell. You're ready for what's next.

The Corsair Quartermaster #123

Defeat the Corsair Quartermaster in the island's jungle well to the south-west of Mudbrook, then return to Foreman Quill in the stilt hall in Mudbrook.

Goal Defeat 1 Corsair QuartermasterTurn in to Foreman Quill
The Quartermaster is down. Only the Captain is left now, and you know where he waits.

Ghost Captain Morrow #124

Defeat Ghost Captain Morrow in the island's jungle well to the west of Mudbrook, then return to Foreman Quill in the stilt hall in Mudbrook. Bring friends and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 Ghost Captain MorrowTurn in to Foreman QuillReward 2 Honing Stone, 2 Reroll Stone
The cave is quiet and the seams are ours again. We'll carve your name on the mine wall. West of us, deeper on the island, Deepgrove keeps the oldest forest in Arvan. The Grove Keeper hasn't sent anyone in weeks.
Next: The Road to Deepgrove

Mudbrook Bounty #125

Defeat 12 Bog Toads in the island's jungle south of Mudbrook and report to Forager Nell in the west stilt hall in Mudbrook. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Bog ToadTurn in to Forager Nell
Good hunting. Mudbrook pays its hunters in gold, and the mine pays its miners in more.

The Upper Seams #182

Mine 6 Gold Ore and 4 Raw Jade in the upper caves of the crag and bring them to Prospector Dov. A Tier 2 pickaxe (Temple Bell or better) will do.

Goal Bring 6 Gold Ore, 4 Raw JadeTurn in to Prospector Dov
Clean ore, and you found your way back out. The deep seams are another matter: you'll want a pick that can bite.

Spirit in the Stone #183

Mine 4 Spirit Stone in the crag's caves and bring them to Prospector Dov. Spirit Stone needs a Tier 3 pickaxe: the Oni Pickaxe (Lv 66), sold by Dov.

Goal Bring 4 Spirit StoneTurn in to Prospector Dov
Feel that? They hum. Nobody in Arvan could cut these before the Oni picks.

Moonsilver and Star Iron #184

Mine 3 Moonsilver Ore and 3 Star Iron deep in the crag and bring them to Prospector Dov. Both need a Tier 3 pickaxe; the Star Iron lies lowest.

Goal Bring 3 Moonsilver Ore, 3 Star IronTurn in to Prospector Dov
Moonsilver and star-fallen iron in one haul. The smiths up north will fight over these.

Dragonbone #185

Mine Dragonbone at the bottom of the crag's caves and bring it to Prospector Dov. Only a Tier 4 pickaxe can cut it: the Dragon Claw Pickaxe (Lv 75), sold by Dov.

Goal Bring 1 DragonboneTurn in to Prospector Dov
A dragon died under this crag before there were people to see it. You're a Mosscrag miner now; nobody can say otherwise.

Dov's Order #186

Bring Prospector Dov 5 Spirit Stone from the crag's caves (Tier 3 pickaxe). A one-time order.

Goal Bring 5 Spirit StoneTurn in to Prospector Dov
Five more for the crates.

Roots for the Miners #237

Pick 8 Crimson Ginseng from the ginseng patches in the jungle clearings around Mudbrook and bring them to Herbalist Tamsa at the herb stall on the east side. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Crimson GinsengTurn in to Herbalist Tamsa
Fat roots, every one. The night shift will thank you.

Drowned Crew #238

Defeat 10 Drowned Corsairs north-west of Mudbrook, then report to Hunt Warden Bryn in the ledger porch on the north-east side in Mudbrook. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Drowned CorsairTurn in to Hunt Warden Bryn
Ten corsairs back in the mud. The carts can roll again.

Crocodile Bank #239

Defeat 10 Mire Crocodiles south-east of Mudbrook, then report to Hunt Warden Bryn in the ledger porch on the north-east side in Mudbrook. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Mire CrocodileTurn in to Hunt Warden Bryn
Ten crocodiles. The bank is ours again.

Golem Cores #240

Bring 3 Moss Golem Cores to Hunt Warden Bryn in the ledger porch on the north-east side in Mudbrook. The Moss Golems north-east of Mudbrook drop them. A one-time job.

Goal Bring 3 Moss Golem CoreTurn in to Hunt Warden Bryn
Three cores, still warm. The deep shafts will have light again.

Lunch for the Foreman #241

Take Cook Branwen's pie to Foreman Quill in his office in the stilt hall in the middle of Mudbrook.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Foreman Quill
I forgot again, didn't I. Tell Branwen thank you, and that I'll eat it now. Truly.

Bog Moss #242

Bring 5 Bog Mosses to Track Guard Idris on the mine track at the south edge in Mudbrook. The Bog Toads south of Mudbrook drop them.

Goal Bring 5 Bog MossTurn in to Track Guard Idris
Five clumps. Smells awful. Good work.

A Lucky Stone #243

Take Little Dilys's lucky stone to Forager Nell in the west stilt hall of Mudbrook.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Forager Nell
A lucky stone from Dilys? I'll keep it in my boot. Tell her I'll come home every night, promise.
