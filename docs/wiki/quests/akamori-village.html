Akamori Village quests

Lv 43-62. A red-roofed coastal village and the last story village. The volcano and Fire Temple lie to its north. 16 quests.

The Red Forest #62

Harbormaster Umi has had no boats from Akamori, the red-roofed village on the mainland north-west of Kinoko Isle, for weeks. Cross back to the mainland and follow the shore north-west to the village, then find Headwoman Akane in the square. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Headwoman Akane
Umi sent you? Then the island still thinks of us. Welcome to Akamori, traveller. You came at a bad time, or a good one: the forest has turned on us.

Jaws in the Marsh #63

Defeat 8 Jade Marsh Crocodiles on the marshy shore south-west of Akamori, then return to Hunter Kaede at the west entrance.

Goal Defeat 8 Jade Marsh CrocodileTurn in to Hunter Kaede
Eight! The fishers can use the shore again. You fight like you've done this before.

Ink in the Canopy #64

Defeat 8 Ink Mantises in the forest west of Akamori, past the west entrance, and report to Hunter Kaede.

Goal Defeat 8 Ink MantisTurn in to Hunter Kaede
The trails west are clear. I owe you a meal, and Hoshi owes you coin for those blades.

Jade-Backed Beasts #65

Defeat 3 Jadeback Pangolins in the rocky hills north-west of Akamori, then return to Hunter Kaede.

Goal Defeat 3 Jadeback PangolinTurn in to Hunter Kaede
Three of them? I've only ever brought one down, and it took me a day. Headwoman Akane should hear about you.

Bloodcap Spawn #66

Defeat 10 Bloodcap Toads along the river just north-west of Akamori and return to Headwoman Akane.

Goal Defeat 10 Bloodcap ToadTurn in to Headwoman Akane
The river runs clean again. Those toads were never red before the forest changed.

Ghostfire in the Trees #67

Put out 10 Ghostfire Wisps in the forest north-east of Akamori, toward the coast, then report to Headwoman Akane.

Goal Defeat 10 Ghostfire WispTurn in to Headwoman Akane
No more pale fires in the trees. Now the children will sleep, and so will I.

Wardens Gone Scarlet #68

Fell 4 Scarlet Wardens in the deep forest west of Akamori, beyond the mantis trails. When one plants its roots, step out of the circle.

Goal Defeat 4 Scarlet WardenTurn in to Headwoman Akane
The wardens kept this forest for a thousand years. Something on the peaks turned them. I think you know where you are going next.

The Ember Tiger #69

Bring down the Ember Tiger on the rocky slopes north of Akamori, then return to Headwoman Akane.

Goal Defeat 1 Ember TigerTurn in to Headwoman Akane
The tiger that burned our charcoal kilns... gone. But it was guarding something up there. It was guarding the way to the shrine.

The Gilded Shishi #70

Defeat the Gilded Shishi, the corrupted guardian of the old shrine on the grey peak north of Akamori, and return to Headwoman Akane.

Goal Defeat 1 Gilded ShishiTurn in to Headwoman AkaneReward 2 Honing Stone
The guardian is at rest, and the forest is ours again. Akamori will remember you. Take these stones; the smiths of Greywall will know what to do with them. The fire mountains north are all that is left now.

Red Forest Bounty #71

Defeat 12 Bloodcap Toads along the river north-west of Akamori and report to Hunter Kaede. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Bloodcap ToadTurn in to Hunter Kaede
Good hunting. Akamori pays its hunters fairly.

Smoke over the Mountain #72

Climb the volcano north of Akamori. Defeat 8 Magma Slimes on its south slopes and 6 Cinder Imps on the south-west slopes, then return to Headwoman Akane.

Goal Defeat 6 Cinder Imp; Defeat 8 Magma SlimeTurn in to Headwoman Akane
So the mountain is waking. Slimes of living rock, imps that throw fire... The old songs said this would come, and that it starts at the Fire Temple.

Ash and Obsidian #73

Go higher up the volcano north of Akamori. Defeat 6 Ash Hounds on the west slopes by the red lava river and 6 Obsidian Salamanders along the banks of the central lava flow, then return to Headwoman Akane.

Goal Defeat 6 Ash Hound; Defeat 6 Obsidian SalamanderTurn in to Headwoman Akane
The hounds hunt in packs now, and the salamanders crawl further down every night. Something up there is calling them.

The Crater's Watch #74

Defeat 6 Ember Wraiths around the crater on the north-east of the volcano and 2 Lava Golems on the north slopes, on the way to the Fire Temple, then return to Headwoman Akane.

Goal Defeat 6 Ember Wraith; Defeat 2 Lava GolemTurn in to Headwoman Akane
With the watchers gone, the road to the Fire Temple is open. Only its gatekeeper remains.

The Gatekeeper #75

Defeat the Hellfire Alpha, the great hound guarding the gate of the Fire Temple on the north side of the volcano, then return to Headwoman Akane.

Goal Defeat 1 Hellfire AlphaTurn in to Headwoman Akane
The gate is yours. Rest, heal, and gather your friends. Inside the temple, the mountain's own creatures wait.

The Fire Temple #76

Enter the Fire Temple through its gate on the north side of the volcano. Defeat the Sulfur Slime King and the Sulfur Colossus on the temple floor. When both fall, the hunt for the Lava Wyrm begins at once: you do not need to return to Akamori.

Goal Defeat 1 Sulfur Colossus; Defeat 1 Sulfur Slime KingTurn in to Headwoman Akane
Two of its guardians have fallen. Now only the heart of the mountain is left: the Lava Wyrm that sleeps at the back of the temple. Gather your strongest friends and every potion you carry, and when it rears up, run from the flames.
Next: The Lava Wyrm

The Lava Wyrm #77

Defeat the Lava Wyrm at the back of the Fire Temple, north of Akamori, then return to Headwoman Akane. It is the strongest creature in Arvan; bring friends and potions, and run from the flames when it rears up.

Goal Defeat 1 Lava WyrmTurn in to Headwoman AkaneReward 4 Honing Stone, 4 Reroll Stone
The mountain is quiet. For the first time since the red forest turned, Akamori sleeps without smoke on the wind. Every village from Valemont to here owes you. Take these: the smiths will know what to do with them.
