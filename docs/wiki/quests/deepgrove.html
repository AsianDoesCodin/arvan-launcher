Deepgrove quests

Lv 60-80. A jungle town on the south-west island. 15 quests.

The Road to Deepgrove #126

Foreman Quill has asked you to carry word to Deepgrove, in the deep grove about 250 blocks west of Mudbrook. Find Grove Keeper Sylva there. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Grove Keeper Sylva
Foreman Quill sent you? Then you are welcome in Deepgrove. The grove's guardian has been poisoned by shadow, and the shadow spreads through everything it guards.

Shadow Mantises #127

Defeat 8 Shadow Mantises in the deep grove north-west of Deepgrove, then return to Grove Keeper Sylva in Deepgrove.

Goal Defeat 8 Shadow MantisTurn in to Grove Keeper Sylva
That's 8 fewer. The people of Deepgrove can breathe a little.

Spore Slimes #128

Defeat 8 Spore Slimes in the deep grove well to the north of Deepgrove, then return to Tracker Ash in Deepgrove.

Goal Defeat 8 Spore SlimeTurn in to Tracker Ash
8 of them, and not a scratch on you? Deepgrove is lucky you came.

Grove Wisps #129

Defeat 8 Grove Wisps in the deep grove south of Deepgrove, then return to Tracker Ash in Deepgrove.

Goal Defeat 8 Grove WispTurn in to Tracker Ash
Good work. The ground out there is ours again, for now.

Ancient Ents #130

Defeat 3 Ancient Ents in the deep grove south-west of Deepgrove, then return to Grove Keeper Sylva in Deepgrove.

Goal Defeat 3 Ancient EntTurn in to Grove Keeper Sylva
3 of those brutes. Even the old hunters never managed that. You're ready for what's next.

The Shadow Tiger #131

Defeat the Shadow Tiger in the deep grove well to the south of Deepgrove, then return to Grove Keeper Sylva in Deepgrove.

Goal Defeat 1 Shadow TigerTurn in to Grove Keeper Sylva
It's down! I saw the smoke from here. Only one thing is left out there now, and you know what it is.

The Corrupted Grove Guardian #132

Defeat the Corrupted Grove Guardian in the deep grove well to the south-east of Deepgrove, then return to Grove Keeper Sylva in Deepgrove. Bring friends and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 Corrupted Grove GuardianTurn in to Grove Keeper SylvaReward 2 Honing Stone, 2 Reroll Stone
The shadow is gone and the tree is singing. Go with the grove's blessing. Back on the mainland, north of Greywall, the fortress of Stonecrest holds the northern pass, and its walls are cracking. Find Castellan Durgan.
Next: The Road to Stonecrest

Good hunting. Deepgrove pays its hunters fairly.

Lilies for the Shrine #247

Still glowing. The shrine will burn bright tonight.
