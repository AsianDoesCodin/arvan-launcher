Rustcliff quests

Lv 60-80. A kingdom with a purple castle on red cliffs. 19 quests.

The Road to Rustcliff #94

Chieftain Yuki has asked you to carry word to Rustcliff, the castle kingdom on the red cliffs about 1100 blocks west of Frostmere. Go through the castle gate and present yourself to King Aurelian in the throne room. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to King Aurelian
Chieftain Yuki sent you? Then you are welcome at my court. Rustcliff has stood on these cliffs for three hundred years, and now the sea is trying to pull it down: beasts climb up from the shore, and something under the cliffs is cracking the castle walls. Ser Gareth commands my guard at the castle gate. Start with him.
