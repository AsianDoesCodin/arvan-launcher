The Outpost quests

Lv 1-25. The allied outpost beyond Valemont's portal, home of Quartermaster Mara. 9 quests.

A Place at the Outpost #12

Meet Quartermaster Mara beyond Valemont's portal and receive her starter supplies. Food and medicine matter here too.

Goal Talk to someoneTurn in to Quartermaster MaraReward 1 Outpost Fishing Rod, 1 bread, 1 apple
Mara welcomes you to the outpost. Ask her about supper from the river.

Supper from the River #13

Fish the river below the outpost and bring Quartermaster Mara 3 cod of any rarity. Keep your other catches.

Goal Bring 3 codTurn in to Quartermaster MaraReward 1 bread, 1 apple
The outpost has supper. Ask Mara about the nearby Greenleaf patches.

What the Trail Provides #14

Gather 4 Greenleaf Herbs from the safe patches near the outpost and bring them to Mara. Each patch regrows after 90 seconds; the patches are shared by all travelers.

Goal Bring 4 Greenleaf HerbTurn in to Quartermaster MaraReward 2 potion
Mara puts the Greenleaf to work and gives you two basic healing potions.

The Missing Field Bag #15

Find Mara's field bag by the trees beside the river, past the Greenleaf slope below the outpost, then bring it back. You can recover it once.

Goal Bring 1 Mara's Field BagTurn in to Quartermaster Mara
Mara is glad to have the bag back. Speak with her about your place at the outpost.

Mara's Order #16

Quartermaster Mara has an order for you. Take her commissions to the outpost smith and have your full starter set forged from the Posta drops and your forage.

Goal Talk to someoneTurn in to Quartermaster Mara
You have earned a place at the outpost. Take your commissions (4 armour pieces and your weapon) to the smith for your class and path, and forge your set.

River Supply Order #17

Optional: fish the river below the outpost and bring Mara 5 cod of any rarity.

Goal Bring 5 codTurn in to Quartermaster Mara
A fresh delivery for the outpost stores.

Greenleaf Supply Order #18

Optional: bring Mara 6 Greenleaf Herbs.

Goal Bring 6 Greenleaf HerbTurn in to Quartermaster Mara
Mara has more herbs for the medicine shelf.

A Horse for the Road #37

Take the outpost’s old horse from Quartermaster Mara.

Goal Talk to someoneTurn in to Quartermaster MaraReward 1 Outpost Horse
There. She is old and she will never win a race, but she beats walking. Right-click the token to add her to your Pets page, then summon her from the Pets page of your menu. Sprint to make her run.

The Road to Redroof #38

Take the outpost's old horse and ride south to Redroof Village. Give Quartermaster Mara's letter to Elder Wen at the village hall. Right-click the horse token to add her to your Pets page, then summon her from the Pets page of your menu. Sprint to make her run.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Elder Wen
A letter from Mara? "Steady, honest, and doesn't complain." That is high praise, from her. Redroof could use someone like that. Rest your horse, then speak with me.
