Redspire quests

Lv 60-80. A grey castle with red spires and a crypt king. 15 quests.

The Road to Redspire #150

Mayor Edric has asked you to carry word to Redspire, in the border woods about 250 blocks west of Pinecross. Find Margrave Aldous there. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Margrave Aldous
Mayor Edric sent you? Then you are welcome in Redspire. The crypt under the castle has opened. The first king of Redspire walks again, and his dead guards with him.

Crypt Scorpions #151

Defeat 8 Crypt Scorpions in the border woods south of Redspire, then return to Margrave Aldous in Redspire.

Goal Defeat 8 Crypt ScorpionTurn in to Margrave Aldous
That's 8 fewer. The people of Redspire can breathe a little.

Moat Lizards #152

Defeat 8 Moat Lizards in the border woods west of Redspire, then return to Ser Roland in Redspire.

Goal Defeat 8 Moat LizardTurn in to Ser Roland
8 of them, and not a scratch on you? Redspire is lucky you came.

Tomb Guards #153

Defeat 8 Tomb Guards in the border woods north-west of Redspire, then return to Ser Roland in Redspire.

Goal Defeat 8 Tomb GuardTurn in to Ser Roland
Good work. The ground out there is ours again, for now.

Rampart Golems #154

Defeat 3 Rampart Golems in the border woods north-east of Redspire, then return to Margrave Aldous in Redspire.

Goal Defeat 3 Rampart GolemTurn in to Margrave Aldous
3 of those brutes. Even the old hunters never managed that. You're ready for what's next.

The Gate Lion #155

Defeat the Gate Lion in the border woods well to the south-west of Redspire, then return to Margrave Aldous in Redspire.

Goal Defeat 1 Gate LionTurn in to Margrave Aldous
It's down! I saw the smoke from here. Only one thing is left out there now, and you know what it is.

The Tomb King #156

Defeat the Tomb King in the border woods well to the north of Redspire, then return to Margrave Aldous in Redspire. Bring friends and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 Tomb KingTurn in to Margrave AldousReward 2 Honing Stone, 2 Reroll Stone
The crypt is sealed and the old king sleeps. Redspire owes you its walls. North, past the last trees, the road crosses the sand to Sunscar, the city of the sun. Sultana Zahra rules there; tell her the Margrave sent you.
Next: The Road to Sunscar

Redspire Bounty #157

Defeat 12 Crypt Scorpions in the border woods south of Redspire and report to Ser Roland. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Crypt ScorpionTurn in to Ser Roland
Good hunting. Redspire pays its hunters fairly.

Ivy for the Night Watch #277

Pick 8 Dragonvein Ivy from the ivy patches in the woods around Redspire and bring them to Herbalist Clemence at the herb stall with the brewing stands on the north side of the courtyard. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Dragonvein IvyTurn in to Herbalist Clemence
Still pulsing, every leaf. The night watch will be wide awake till dawn.

Moat Lizard Cull #278

Defeat 10 Moat Lizards west of Redspire, then report to Hunt Warden Gerold at the planter counter of stall C on the south row in Redspire. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Moat LizardTurn in to Hunt Warden Gerold
Ten lizards. The shepherds can sleep again.

The Dead Patrol #279

Defeat 10 Tomb Guards north-west of Redspire, then report to Hunt Warden Gerold at the planter counter of stall C on the south row in Redspire. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Tomb GuardTurn in to Hunt Warden Gerold
Ten tomb guards. Their patrol is a little shorter tonight.

Rampart Hearts #280

Bring 3 Rampart Hearts to Hunt Warden Gerold at the planter counter of stall C on the south row in Redspire. The Rampart Golems north-east of Redspire drop them. A one-time job.

Goal Bring 3 Rampart HeartTurn in to Hunt Warden Gerold
Three hearts, still warm. Wendel will have the east wall mended by spring.

Supper for the Wall #281

Take Cook Hester's pot of stew to Ser Roland at the knights' post on the west side of the courtyard in Redspire.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Ser Roland
Hester's stew? I'm not hungry. ...I'm hungry. Tell her the knights of Redspire thank her.

Crypt Stingers #282

Bring 5 Crypt Stingers to Sentry Osbert at the gatehouse on the south road in Redspire. The Crypt Scorpions south of Redspire drop them.

Goal Bring 5 Crypt StingerTurn in to Sentry Osbert
Five stingers. I'll nail one over the gate as a warning to the rest.

A Buckle from the Moat #283

Take Little Alba's old buckle to Smith Wendel at the forge on the north side of the courtyard in Redspire.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Smith Wendel
A knight's buckle, and an old one. See the red tower, worn nearly flat? Tell Alba I'll clean it up and put it on a belt for her.
