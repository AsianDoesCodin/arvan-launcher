Quests

264 quests across 19 areas. The story runs from Valemont through Redroof and Akamori, then twelve journey villages, with repeatable town quests along the way.

Valemont (Act 1)

Lv 1-10 · 4 quests

The starting area. Elder Posta sends you after goblins and your first crafting materials.

The Outpost

Lv 1-25 · 9 quests

The allied outpost beyond Valemont's portal, home of Quartermaster Mara.

Redroof Village

Lv 15-25 · 12 quests

The red-roofed village east of the central castle city.

Shakokonai City

Lv 25-35 · 6 quests

A big cherry-blossom city in the east, where mining is introduced.

Greywall Citadel

Lv 31-42 · 10 quests

A grey stone fortress west of Redroof. Enter by the north gate.

Kinoko Isle

Any level · 14 quests

A peaceful mushroom island for fishing, herbs and ore.

Akamori Village

Lv 43-62 · 16 quests

A red-roofed coastal village and the last story village. The volcano and Fire Temple lie to its north.
