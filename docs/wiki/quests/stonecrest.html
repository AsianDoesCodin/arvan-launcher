Stonecrest quests

Lv 60-80. A stone hill fortress. 15 quests.

The Road to Stonecrest #134

Grove Keeper Sylva has asked you to carry word to Stonecrest, in the highlands about 1400 blocks north-east of Deepgrove. Find Castellan Durgan there. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Castellan Durgan
Grove Keeper Sylva sent you? Then you are welcome in Stonecrest. The peaks have come alive. Crag beasts walk down the slopes every night, and something under the hill is shaking our walls.

Crag Golems #135

Defeat 8 Crag Golems in the highlands south of Stonecrest, then return to Castellan Durgan in Stonecrest.

Goal Defeat 8 Crag GolemTurn in to Castellan Durgan
That's 8 fewer. The people of Stonecrest can breathe a little.

Crag Basilisks #136

Defeat 8 Crag Basilisks in the highlands south-west of Stonecrest, then return to Captain Kell in Stonecrest.

Goal Defeat 8 Crag BasiliskTurn in to Captain Kell
8 of them, and not a scratch on you? Stonecrest is lucky you came.

Peak Imps #137

Defeat 8 Peak Imps in the highlands south-east of Stonecrest, then return to Captain Kell in Stonecrest.

Goal Defeat 8 Peak ImpTurn in to Captain Kell
Good work. The ground out there is ours again, for now.

Stonecrest Sentinels #138

Defeat 3 Stonecrest Sentinels in the highlands east of Stonecrest, then return to Castellan Durgan in Stonecrest.

Goal Defeat 3 Stonecrest SentinelTurn in to Castellan Durgan
3 of those brutes. Even the old hunters never managed that. You're ready for what's next.

The Crag Wyrm #139

Defeat the Crag Wyrm in the highlands well to the north-west of Stonecrest, then return to Castellan Durgan in Stonecrest.

Goal Defeat 1 Crag WyrmTurn in to Castellan Durgan
It's down! I saw the smoke from here. Only one thing is left out there now, and you know what it is.

The Mountain Tortoise #140

Defeat the Mountain Tortoise in the highlands well to the west of Stonecrest, then return to Castellan Durgan in Stonecrest. Bring friends and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 Mountain TortoiseTurn in to Castellan DurganReward 3 Honing Stone
The walls are standing and the hill is still. Take these stones: you've earned the best we have. West through the pine woods lies Pinecross, a market town under a storm that won't break. Find Mayor Edric.
Next: The Road to Pinecross

Stonecrest Bounty #141

Defeat 12 Crag Golems in the highlands south of Stonecrest and report to Captain Kell. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Crag GolemTurn in to Captain Kell
Good hunting. Stonecrest pays its hunters fairly.

Moss for the Night Watch #257

Pick 8 Starfall Moss from the grassy slopes below the walls of Stonecrest and bring them to Herbalist Isla in the flower garden on the north-west side of the square. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Starfall MossTurn in to Herbalist Isla
Still glowing, every one. The night watch will see the crag beasts coming.

Basilisk Cull #258

Defeat 10 Crag Basilisks south-west of Stonecrest, then report to Hunt Warden Gregor in the archers' hall on the east side of the square in Stonecrest. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Crag BasiliskTurn in to Hunt Warden Gregor
Ten basilisks. The scouts can feel their feet again.

Imp Patrol #259

Defeat 10 Peak Imps south-east of Stonecrest, then report to Hunt Warden Gregor in the archers' hall on the east side of the square in Stonecrest. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Peak ImpTurn in to Hunt Warden Gregor
Ten imps. Tonight the wall walk might even be quiet.

Sentinel Plate #260

Bring 3 Sentinel Plates to Hunt Warden Gregor in the archers' hall on the east side of the square in Stonecrest. The Stonecrest Sentinels east of Stonecrest drop them. A one-time job.

Goal Bring 3 Sentinel PlateTurn in to Hunt Warden Gregor
Three plates. Brann will have them hammered into shields by morning.

Stew for the Forge #261

Take Cook Moira's pot of stew to Smith Brann at his forge on the north-west side of the square in Stonecrest.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Smith Brann
Moira's stew? Ha. The forge can wait five minutes. Tell her thank you.

Golem Cores #262

Bring 5 Crag Golem Cores to Gatewarden Hamish at the top of the steps at the west gate of Stonecrest. The Crag Golems south of Stonecrest drop them.

Goal Bring 5 Crag Golem CoreTurn in to Gatewarden Hamish
Five cores, still warm. I'll set one on the gatepost to warn the rest.

An Arrow for the Captain #263

Take Little Ewan's home-made arrow to Captain Kell in the archers' hall on the east side of the square in Stonecrest.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Captain Kell
Ewan's arrow? Straight as a rule, and the feather's on right. Tell him he can carry quivers on the wall walk. Carry, mind.
