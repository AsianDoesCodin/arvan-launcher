Kinoko Isle quests

Any level. A peaceful mushroom island for fishing, herbs and ore. 14 quests.

Island Waters #48

Catch 4 Spotted Capfish anywhere in the waters of Kinoko Isle (the pond between the mushroom houses counts) and bring them to Harbormaster Umi at the pond.

Goal Bring 4 Spotted CapfishTurn in to Harbormaster Umi
Four capfish, and you kept your fingers. You've got the knack. Fishing here is harder than on the mainland, but it pays.

Glow in the Deep #49

Catch 2 Glowcap Eels around Kinoko Isle and bring them to Harbormaster Umi. Eels only bite on a Coral Rod (Lv 20) or better; Umi sells one.

Goal Bring 2 Glowcap EelTurn in to Harbormaster Umi
Look at them glow! The cooks in Shakokonai will fight over these.

Moonscale #50

Catch a Moonscale Koi around Kinoko Isle and bring it to Harbormaster Umi. Koi only bite on a Moonsilver Rod (Lv 35) or better.

Goal Bring 1 Moonscale KoiTurn in to Harbormaster Umi
A Moonscale. I've caught three in thirty years. Keep fishing these waters: there's a golden one out there too.

The Day's Catch #51

Bring Harbormaster Umi 6 Mycelium Carp from the waters of Kinoko Isle. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 6 Mycelium CarpTurn in to Harbormaster Umi
Fresh carp for the smokehouse.

Salt and Glass #52

Gather 5 Saltglass Kelp on the south-west shore of Kinoko Isle and bring them to Herbalist Hana by the mushroom houses.

Goal Bring 5 Saltglass KelpTurn in to Herbalist HanaReward 1 Bamboo Sickle
Lovely bulbs. Here: a sickle of my own. Hold it while you pick and you'll get more from every patch.

Caps Under the Caps #53

Gather 6 Sakura Dewcaps. They grow under the giant mushrooms all over Kinoko Isle. Bring them to Herbalist Hana.

Goal Bring 6 Sakura DewcapTurn in to Herbalist Hana
Pink and plump. These make the best tea in Arvan.

Lotus on the Pond #54

Pick 3 Moonpetal Lotus from the pond between the mushroom houses (they float on the water) and bring them to Herbalist Hana.

Goal Bring 3 Moonpetal LotusTurn in to Herbalist Hana
Careful with the petals... perfect. Umi will grumble that you scared his fish.

Hana's Order #55

Bring Herbalist Hana 4 Sakura Dewcaps and 2 Saltglass Kelp from Kinoko Isle. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 4 Sakura Dewcap, 2 Saltglass KelpTurn in to Herbalist Hana
Just what my kettle needed.

Strike the Isle #56

Mine 6 Copper Ore and 4 Iron Ore from the ore nodes around Kinoko Isle and bring them to Prospector Gen. Any pickaxe works; Gen sells them.

Goal Bring 6 Copper Ore, 4 Iron OreTurn in to Prospector Gen
Good honest ore. The mushrooms grow on it, you know. Or it grows on them. Nobody's sure.

A Silver Seam #57

Mine 4 Silver Ore from the nodes on Kinoko Isle and bring them to Prospector Gen. Silver needs a Tier 2 pickaxe: Gen's Hearth Starter Pickaxe or better.

Goal Bring 4 Silver OreTurn in to Prospector Gen
Bright as a new coin. You're a proper miner now.

Green Stone, Gold Stone #58

Mine 2 Raw Jade and 2 Gold Ore on Kinoko Isle and bring them to Prospector Gen.

Goal Bring 2 Raw Jade, 2 Gold OreTurn in to Prospector Gen
Jade and gold. Now, about those strange glowing stones on the island's edges... come back when you have a pickaxe that can crack them.

Gen's Order #59

Bring Prospector Gen 8 Iron Ore from Kinoko Isle. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Iron OreTurn in to Prospector Gen
Iron for the island's tools. Much obliged.

A Little Help #60

Bring Keeper Nami 3 Spotted Capfish, 3 Sakura Dewcaps and 3 Copper Ore, all from Kinoko Isle, to show the sprites you work the island.

Goal Bring 3 Spotted Capfish, 3 Sakura Dewcap, 3 Copper OreTurn in to Keeper Nami
The sprites like you. If you ever want one by your side, I can find you a Spore Sprite: it helps with the rod, the pick and the herbs.

Tempered by the Isle #307

Mine 10 Iron Ore and 5 Silver Ore on Kinoko Isle, hunt 5 Grave Iron Shards near Shakokonai, and bring them to Hearthsmith Iwao by Prospector Gen's stall.

Goal Bring 10 Iron Ore, 5 Silver Ore, 5 Grave Iron ShardTurn in to Hearthsmith IwaoReward 1 Iron Miner's Gloves
Good ore, and you fought for the shards. Here, wear these gloves while you dig. Every strike gives a little more.
