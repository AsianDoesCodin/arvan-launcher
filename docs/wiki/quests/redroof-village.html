Redroof Village quests

Lv 15-25. The red-roofed village east of the central castle city. 12 quests.

Breaking Ground #19

Smith Guo needs ore for his forge. Mine 3 Copper Ore and 3 Iron Ore with a pickaxe, in the meadow south of Redroof, and bring them back to Smith Guo.

Goal Bring 3 Copper Ore, 3 Iron OreTurn in to Smith Guo
Guo turns the ore over in his hands. Honest work. This will keep the forge burning.

Toads in the Paddies #20

Bring 6 toad glands from the Lotus Toads in Redroof's rice paddies to Farmer Huo; you keep them. Step aside when a toad's throat swells: it only strikes straight ahead.

Goal Bring 6 Toad GlandTurn in to Farmer Huo
The paddies are quiet again. Miller Bao has been grumbling about his waterwheel.

The Mill Stands Still #21

Bring Miller Bao 6 Mantis Blades from the Bamboo Mantises in the grove upstream, so he can mend his waterwheel.

Goal Bring 6 Mantis BladeTurn in to Miller Bao
The waterwheel turns again. But Woodcutter Dao went up to his forest camp four days ago and has not come back down, and Elder Wen is worried. His camp is in the woods past the river: look for the lean-to and the stacked logs.
Next: The Smith's Test

The Smith's Test #22

Smith Guo wants to see what you can make. Craft any piece of gear from his Redroof recipes (it must suit your class and path), then tell him.

Goal Follow the quest textTurn in to Smith Guo
Guo checks the stitching and the weight, and nods. You have the makings of a smith.

Thornbacks at the Camp #23

Find Woodcutter Dao at his camp in the forest beyond the river and bring him 3 thornback shingles from the Thornback Pangolins around it; you keep them. When one curls into a ball, back off until it unrolls.

Goal Bring 3 Pangolin ScaleTurn in to Woodcutter DaoReward 4 Redroof Armor Commission, 1 Redroof Weapon Commission
The thornbacks are gone. Dao thinks they were running from something. For keeping the camp safe, Redroof gives you four armour commissions and a weapon commission: take them to Smith Guo first. Elder Wen already knows what scared them: the Blossom Serpent in the blossom trees on the east road, outside King Hayato's castle. Slay it, then report to the King.
Next: The Blossom Serpent

The Blossom Serpent #24

Slay the Blossom Serpent in the blossom trees on the east road, outside King Hayato's castle, then report to King Hayato in Shakokonai.

Goal Defeat 1 Blossom SerpentTurn in to King Hayato
The serpent at my gates is dead? Then Elder Wen chose well. Shakokonai has work for a hunter like you.

Grease for the Wheel #25

Bring Miller Bao 6 Toad Glands from the Lotus Toads in the paddies; he boils them down into grease for the waterwheel's axle. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 6 Toad GlandTurn in to Miller Bao
The axle turns smooth and quiet.

Lingzhi for the Shelf #26

Gather 6 Jade Lingzhi from the shady patches around Redroof and bring them to Herbalist Mei. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 6 Jade LingzhiTurn in to Herbalist Mei
Mei's shelves are stocked.

The Old Whisker #27

Catch 5 salmon of any rarity and bring them to Old Jiang at the pond to prove you can fish.

Goal Bring 5 salmonTurn in to Old Jiang
Jiang told you where the Old Whisker sleeps. One day, perhaps.

The Lost Pup #28

Find Pip's collar bell in the reeds along the stream and bring it back to Little Tao.

Goal Bring 1 Pip's Collar BellTurn in to Little TaoReward 1 potion, 1 potion
Pip's bell jingles again.

Thornback Shingles #29

Bring Woodcutter Dao 3 Pangolin Scales from the Thornback Pangolins around his camp; he shingles his cabin roof with them before winter. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 3 Pangolin ScaleTurn in to Woodcutter Dao
Dao's cabin will stay dry this winter.

Mantis Bounty #30

Defeat 10 Bamboo Mantises in the bamboo grove upstream and report to Lookout Ren. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 10 Bamboo MantisTurn in to Lookout Ren
The grove is quieter, for now.
