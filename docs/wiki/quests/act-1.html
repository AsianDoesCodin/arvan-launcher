Valemont (Act 1) quests

Lv 1-10. The starting area. Elder Posta sends you after goblins and your first crafting materials. 4 quests.

Elder Posta #1

Speak to Elder Posta inside Valemont.

Goal Talk to someoneTurn in to Elder Posta
Speak to Elder Posta again to begin helping the village.

Goblins Outside Valemont #2

Defeat 6 Goblins in the goblin grounds outside Valemont, then return to Elder Posta. Keep any Weak Pelts you find.

Goal Defeat 6 GoblinTurn in to Elder PostaReward 1 Starter Weapon Commission, 4 Starter Armor Commission
Six goblins dealt with. Take your starter weapon commission and four armor commissions to the blacksmith in Valemont for a full Tier 1 set before the packwolf hunt.

The Packwolf Threat #3

Defeat 6 Packwolves near the rocky wolf den, then return to Elder Posta. Each Packwolf drops a Weak Pelt; save at least 4 for later.

Goal Defeat 6 PackwolfTurn in to Elder Posta
Six packwolves down. Keep your Weak Pelts and speak to Elder Posta again about the Corrupted Goblins.

Six Manacores #4

Collect 6 Manacores from Corrupted Goblins in the corrupted grounds and show them to Elder Posta; you keep them, they are crafting material. Each Corrupted Goblin drops one core. Afterward, visit Quartermaster Mara at the allied outpost beyond Valemont's portal.

Goal Bring 6 ManacoreTurn in to Elder Posta
Your work for Valemont is complete and Posta's blessing is yours. Cross the portal to the allied outpost and meet Quartermaster Mara for a welcoming supply assignment.
