Hillwatch quests

Lv 60-80. First of the twelve journey villages. 15 quests.

The Road to Hillwatch #78

Headwoman Akane has asked you to carry word to Hillwatch, in the grassy hills about 700 blocks north-west of Akamori. Find Warden Haru there. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Warden Haru
Headwoman Akane sent you? Then you are welcome in Hillwatch. Since the mountain in the south-east fell quiet, every beast that fled the fire has come north into our hills.

Hill Jackals #79

Defeat 8 Hill Jackals in the grassy hills south-east of Hillwatch, then return to Warden Haru in Hillwatch.

Goal Defeat 8 Hill JackalTurn in to Warden Haru
That's 8 fewer. The people of Hillwatch can breathe a little.

Thistle Mantises #80

Defeat 8 Thistle Mantises in the grassy hills north-west of Hillwatch, then return to Scout Rin in Hillwatch.

Goal Defeat 8 Thistle MantisTurn in to Scout Rin
8 of them, and not a scratch on you? Hillwatch is lucky you came.

Moss Basilisks #81

Defeat 8 Moss Basilisks in the grassy hills north-east of Hillwatch, then return to Scout Rin in Hillwatch.

Goal Defeat 8 Moss BasiliskTurn in to Scout Rin
Good work. The ground out there is ours again, for now.

Granite Pangolins #82

Defeat 3 Granite Pangolins in the grassy hills west of Hillwatch, then return to Warden Haru in Hillwatch.

Goal Defeat 3 Granite PangolinTurn in to Warden Haru
3 of those brutes. Even the old hunters never managed that. You're ready for what's next.

The Thunder Tiger #83

Defeat the Thunder Tiger in the grassy hills well to the south-west of Hillwatch, then return to Warden Haru in Hillwatch.

Goal Defeat 1 Thunder TigerTurn in to Warden Haru
It's down! I saw the smoke from here. Only one thing is left out there now, and you know what it is.

The Storm Juggernaut #84

Defeat the Storm Juggernaut in the grassy hills well to the south of Hillwatch, then return to Warden Haru in Hillwatch. Bring friends and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 Storm JuggernautTurn in to Warden HaruReward 2 Honing Stone, 2 Reroll Stone
The ridge is quiet and the storm has passed. Hillwatch will hold a feast for you tonight. The road east of the mountains leads on to Frostmere, where the snow never melts; their chieftain sent word asking for help.
Next: The Road to Frostmere

Hillwatch Bounty #85

Defeat 12 Hill Jackals in the grassy hills south-east of Hillwatch and report to Scout Rin. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Hill JackalTurn in to Scout Rin
Good hunting. Hillwatch pays its hunters fairly.

Leaves for the Scouts #197

Pick 8 Cloud Tea Leaves from the tea shrubs on the open hillsides around Hillwatch and bring them to Herbalist Mirelle at the herb stall in the eastern hall. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Cloud Tea LeavesTurn in to Herbalist Mirelle
Silver tips, every one. The scouts will be running the ridge by morning.

Mantis Harvest #198

Defeat 10 Thistle Mantises north-west of Hillwatch, then report to Hunt Warden Garrick in the ledger room of the south hall in Hillwatch. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Thistle MantisTurn in to Hunt Warden Garrick
Ten mantises. The shepherds can mend their fences in peace.

Basilisk Watch #199

Defeat 10 Moss Basilisks north-east of Hillwatch, then report to Hunt Warden Garrick in the ledger room of the south hall in Hillwatch. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Moss BasiliskTurn in to Hunt Warden Garrick
Ten basilisks. No more stone goats, I hope.

Granite Scales #200

Bring 3 Granite Scales to Hunt Warden Garrick in the ledger room of the south hall in Hillwatch. The Granite Pangolins west of Hillwatch drop them. A one-time job.

Goal Bring 3 Granite ScaleTurn in to Hunt Warden Garrick
Three good scales. Odo will be pleased; he's been waiting all month.

Pies for the Scouts #201

Take Cook Pella's basket of pies to Scout Rin in the scouts' room of the big hall north of the market.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Scout Rin
Pella's pies? The scouts will fight over these. Tell her thank you, and that we owe her.

Jackal Pelts #202

Bring 5 Hill Jackal Hides to Roadwarden Hugo at the road post on the south-west road in Hillwatch. The Hill Jackals south-east of Hillwatch drop them.

Goal Bring 5 Hill Jackal HideTurn in to Roadwarden Hugo
Five hides. I'll hang one on the post so the rest of the pack gets the message.

A Map for the Warden #203

Take Little Pim's hand-drawn map of the hills to Warden Haru in the west hall of the courtyard.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Warden Haru
Pim's map? Ha. Every hill, and the jackal dens marked too. Tell the kid I'm pinning it on the wall. Maybe a scout after all.
