Sunscar quests

Lv 60-80. A scorched desert town. 15 quests.

The Road to Sunscar #158

Margrave Aldous has asked you to carry word to Sunscar, in the desert about 300 blocks north-west of Redspire. Find Sultana Zahra there. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Sultana Zahra
Margrave Aldous sent you? Then you are welcome in Sunscar. The Sun Pharaoh, the temple's ancient guardian, has woken angry. The desert burns hotter every day.

Sun Mantises #159

Defeat 8 Sun Mantises in the desert south-east of Sunscar, then return to Sultana Zahra in Sunscar.

Goal Defeat 8 Sun MantisTurn in to Sultana Zahra
That's 8 fewer. The people of Sunscar can breathe a little.

Fire Jackals #160

Defeat 8 Fire Jackals in the desert south of Sunscar, then return to Hunter Karim in Sunscar.

Goal Defeat 8 Fire JackalTurn in to Hunter Karim
8 of them, and not a scratch on you? Sunscar is lucky you came.

Djinn Wisps #161

Defeat 8 Djinn Wisps in the desert north-west of Sunscar, then return to Hunter Karim in Sunscar.

Goal Defeat 8 Djinn WispTurn in to Hunter Karim
Good work. The ground out there is ours again, for now.

Sand Wurms #162

Defeat 3 Sand Wurms in the desert south-west of Sunscar, then return to Sultana Zahra in Sunscar.

Goal Defeat 3 Sand WurmTurn in to Sultana Zahra
3 of those brutes. Even the old hunters never managed that. You're ready for what's next.

The Efreet #163

Defeat the Efreet in the desert well to the east of Sunscar, then return to Sultana Zahra in Sunscar.

Goal Defeat 1 EfreetTurn in to Sultana Zahra
It's down! I saw the smoke from here. Only one thing is left out there now, and you know what it is.

The Sun Pharaoh #164

Defeat the Sun Pharaoh in the desert well to the north-east of Sunscar, then return to Sultana Zahra in Sunscar. Bring friends and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 Sun PharaohTurn in to Sultana ZahraReward 3 Honing Stone, 1 Reroll Stone
The sun is gentle again. Sunscar is in your debt, and I pay my debts. One place is left: the Ashen Oasis, at the heart of the desert to the north-west. Priestess Anahita has seen something there no one else will name.
Next: The Road to Ashen Oasis

Sunscar Bounty #165

Defeat 12 Sun Mantises in the desert south-east of Sunscar and report to Hunter Karim. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Sun MantisTurn in to Hunter Karim
Good hunting. Sunscar pays its hunters fairly.

Blooms for the Heat #287

Pick 8 Sunscale Blooms from the cactus patches in the dunes around Sunscar and bring them to Herbalist Soraya at the herb stall in the north market. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Sunscale BloomTurn in to Herbalist Soraya
Bright orange, every one. The caravan leaves at dawn, and now it will arrive.

The Burning Pack #288

Defeat 10 Fire Jackals south of Sunscar, then report to Hunt Warden Bahram on the open plaza west of the well, facing the well in Sunscar. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Fire JackalTurn in to Hunt Warden Bahram
Ten jackals. The goats can graze without catching fire.

False Wishes #289

Defeat 10 Djinn Wisps north-west of Sunscar, then report to Hunt Warden Bahram on the open plaza west of the well, facing the well in Sunscar. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Djinn WispTurn in to Hunt Warden Bahram
Ten wisps gone. The road is quiet again, and nobody is wishing for anything.

Wurm Teeth #290

Bring 3 Sand Wurm Teeth to Hunt Warden Bahram on the ground floor of the tall tower west of the well in Sunscar. The Sand Wurms south-west of Sunscar drop them. A one-time job.

Goal Bring 3 Sand Wurm ToothTurn in to Hunt Warden Bahram
Three teeth, as sharp as the day they grew. Faris will be grinding all week.

Tea for the Hunter #291

Take Tea Master Malik's flask of cold mint tea to Hunter Karim at his hut on the west side of Sunscar.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Hunter Karim
Cold tea, in this heat? Malik is a wise man. Tell him the hunt owes him a jackal pelt.

Sun Chitin #292

Bring 5 pieces of Sun Chitin to Sun Guard Kaveh on the south road below the palace in Sunscar. The Sun Mantises south-east of Sunscar drop them.

Goal Bring 5 Sun ChitinTurn in to Sun Guard Kaveh
Five shells. I'll hang them on my post; they shine at night and warn the rest.

A Jewel from the Sand #293

Take Little Dina's red stone to Jeweller Nadia just south of the tea house in the middle of Sunscar.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Jeweller Nadia
Ha! Red glass from an old temple lamp, worn smooth by the sand. Worth nothing, and lovely. Tell Dina I'll set it in a ring for her.
