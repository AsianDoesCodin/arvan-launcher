Reedwater quests

Lv 60-80. A stilt fishing village on a lagoon. 19 quests.

The Road to Reedwater #110

Reeve Aldric has asked you to carry word to Reedwater, the fishing village on the lagoon about 450 blocks south-west of Westmarch, where the Thornwood meets the water. Find Elder Mirela on the boardwalks. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Elder Mirela
Reeve Aldric sent you? Then you are welcome in Reedwater. Our boats haven't gone out in a week. The Thornwood around the lagoon has gone wild, and something in it is poisoning the water: the fish are dying, and so will we.

Thorn Mantises #111

Defeat 8 Thorn Mantises in the Thornwood north of Reedwater, then return to Elder Mirela on the boardwalks of Reedwater.

Goal Defeat 8 Thorn MantisTurn in to Elder Mirela
That's 8 fewer. The children can play on the shore again.

Venom Centipedes #112

Defeat 8 Venom Centipedes in the Thornwood north-east of Reedwater, then return to Fisher Tomas on the boardwalk of Reedwater.

Goal Defeat 8 Venom CentipedeTurn in to Fisher Tomas
8 of them, and not a scratch on you? Reedwater is lucky you came.

Dire Wolves #113

Defeat 8 Dire Wolves in the Thornwood north-west of Reedwater, then return to Fisher Tomas on the boardwalk of Reedwater.

Goal Defeat 8 Dire WolfTurn in to Fisher Tomas
Good work. The wolves won't come down to the boats tonight.

Briar Pangolins #114

Defeat 3 Briar Pangolins in the Thornwood well to the south of Reedwater, then return to Elder Mirela on the boardwalks of Reedwater.

Goal Defeat 3 Briar PangolinTurn in to Elder Mirela
3 of those brutes. Even our oldest hunters never managed that. You're ready for what's next.

The Bramble Basilisk #115

Defeat the Bramble Basilisk in the Thornwood well to the east of Reedwater, then return to Elder Mirela on the boardwalks of Reedwater.

Goal Defeat 1 Bramble BasiliskTurn in to Elder Mirela
It's down! The streams already look clearer. Only one thing is left in the Thornwood now, and you know what it is.

The Elder Thornwarden #116

Defeat the Elder Thornwarden in the Thornwood well to the south-east of Reedwater, then return to Elder Mirela on the boardwalks of Reedwater. Bring friends and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 Elder ThornwardenTurn in to Elder MirelaReward 1 Honing Stone, 1 Reroll Stone
The streams are running clear and the fish are biting again. Reedwater will feed you as long as you live. South-west, across the water, Mudbrook sent a bird: its miners dug something up in the Mosscrag mine. Foreman Quill could use you.
Next: The Road to Mudbrook

Reedwater Bounty #117

Defeat 12 Thorn Mantises in the Thornwood north of Reedwater and report to Fisher Tomas on the boardwalk of Reedwater. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Thorn MantisTurn in to Fisher Tomas
Good hunting. Reedwater pays its hunters in gold and smoked fish.

Lagoon Lessons #178

Catch 6 Reed Perch in Reedwater Lagoon (any rod) and bring them to Fisher Tomas on the boardwalk.

Goal Bring 6 Reed PerchTurn in to Fisher Tomas
Six perch, and you didn't fall in. The lagoon fights harder than a river; you'll get the feel of it.

Lights in the Mud #179

Catch 3 Mudlight Eels in Reedwater Lagoon and bring them to Fisher Tomas. Eels only bite on a Lv 50 rod (the Kinoko Master Rod) or better.

Goal Bring 3 Mudlight EelTurn in to Fisher Tomas
Look at them glow. Fen will sell these to every cook between here and Westmarch.

The Elder Carp #180

Catch an Elder Carp in Reedwater Lagoon and bring it to Fisher Tomas. It only bites on the Heron Rod (Lv 65); Fishmonger Fen sells it.

Goal Bring 1 Elder CarpTurn in to Fisher Tomas
The Elder Carp... my grandfather swore it was a story. You're a Reedwater fisher now. And there's a golden koi out there too, they say.

Fen's Order #181

Bring Fishmonger Fen 8 Lagoon Bream from Reedwater Lagoon. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Lagoon BreamTurn in to Fishmonger Fen
Fresh bream for the smokehouse.

Pitchers for the Fever #227

Pick 8 Mirebloom Pitchers from the sandy shore around Reedwater and bring them to Herbalist Linnea in the brewing room on the south boardwalk. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Mirebloom PitcherTurn in to Herbalist Linnea
Whole pitchers, not a drop spilled. Three fishers will be back in their boats by tomorrow.

Centipede Nests #228

Defeat 10 Venom Centipedes north-east of Reedwater, then report to Hunt Warden Osk in the library porch on the west boardwalk in Reedwater. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Venom CentipedeTurn in to Hunt Warden Osk
Ten centipedes. The posts are safe again.

Wolves at Dusk #229

Defeat 10 Dire Wolves north-west of Reedwater, then report to Hunt Warden Osk in the library porch on the west boardwalk in Reedwater. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Dire WolfTurn in to Hunt Warden Osk
Ten wolves. The fishers can walk home in the dark again.

Briar Scales #230

Bring 3 Briar Scales to Hunt Warden Osk in the library porch on the west boardwalk in Reedwater. The Briar Pangolins well to the south of Reedwater drop them. A one-time job.

Goal Bring 3 Briar ScaleTurn in to Hunt Warden Osk
Three scales. Rook will plate a whole boat with these.

Pie for the Fisher #231

Take Innkeeper Hilde's eel pie to Fisher Tomas on the jetty by the fish stall in Reedwater.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Fisher Tomas
Hilde's eel pie? Now that's a catch. Tell her I'll bring her the big one when I land it.

Mantis Chitin #232

Bring 5 Thorn Chitins to Watchman Edvin at the watch house on the east side in Reedwater. The Thorn Mantises north of Reedwater drop them.

Goal Bring 5 Thorn ChitinTurn in to Watchman Edvin
Five pieces. I'll hang them on the watch post as a warning.

A Pearl for the Elder #233

Take Little Saga's pearl to Elder Mirela in her house at the south end of the Reedwater boardwalk.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Elder Mirela
A pearl from Saga? And a good wish, too. Tell her the Elder will keep it safe until it comes true.
