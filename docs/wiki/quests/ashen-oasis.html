Ashen Oasis quests

Lv 60-80. A desert oasis town. 15 quests.

The Road to Ashen Oasis #166

Sultana Zahra has asked you to carry word to Ashen Oasis, in the ashen sands about 300 blocks north-west of Sunscar. Find Priestess Anahita there. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Priestess Anahita
Sultana Zahra sent you? Then you are welcome in Ashen Oasis. The Lord of the Ashen Sands sleeps under the oasis. Everything you have fought since Akamori fled from him. He is waking.

Ash Ghouls #167

Defeat 8 Ash Ghouls in the ashen sands well to the north-east of Ashen Oasis, then return to Priestess Anahita in Ashen Oasis.

Goal Defeat 8 Ash GhoulTurn in to Priestess Anahita
That's 8 fewer. The people of Ashen Oasis can breathe a little.

Oasis Nagas #168

Defeat 8 Oasis Nagas in the ashen sands south of Ashen Oasis, then return to Warden Darius in Ashen Oasis.

Goal Defeat 8 Oasis NagaTurn in to Warden Darius
8 of them, and not a scratch on you? Ashen Oasis is lucky you came.

Ashen Revenants #169

Defeat 8 Ashen Revenants in the ashen sands south-east of Ashen Oasis, then return to Warden Darius in Ashen Oasis.

Goal Defeat 8 Ashen RevenantTurn in to Warden Darius
Good work. The ground out there is ours again, for now.

Ashen Basilisks #170

Defeat 3 Ashen Basilisks in the ashen sands north-east of Ashen Oasis, then return to Priestess Anahita in Ashen Oasis.

Goal Defeat 3 Ashen BasiliskTurn in to Priestess Anahita
3 of those brutes. Even the old hunters never managed that. You're ready for what's next.

The Ash Djinn #171

Defeat the Ash Djinn in the ashen sands well to the east of Ashen Oasis, then return to Priestess Anahita in Ashen Oasis.

Goal Defeat 1 Ash DjinnTurn in to Priestess Anahita
It's down! I saw the smoke from here. Only one thing is left out there now, and you know what it is.

The Serpent King #172

Defeat King Naga, the serpent king, on his throne in the ashen sands south of Ashen Oasis (follow the waypoint), then return to Priestess Anahita. Bring your strongest friends, Lv 80 gear and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 King NagaTurn in to Priestess AnahitaReward 1 Rarity Upgrader, 2 Honing Stone
The serpent king has fallen and the seal under the oasis is open. The first Faced World is yours. Hero of Arvan, your road does not end here: journey to the 2nd Faced World.

Ashen Oasis Bounty #173

Defeat 12 Ash Ghouls in the ashen sands well to the north-east of Ashen Oasis and report to Warden Darius. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Ash GhoulTurn in to Warden Darius
Good hunting. Ashen Oasis pays its hunters fairly.

Peppers for the Forge #297

Pick 8 Phoenix Peppers from the pepper bushes on the open sand around Ashen Oasis and bring them to Herbalist Mehrnaz at the herb stall in the round bazaar south of the plaza. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Phoenix PepperTurn in to Herbalist Mehrnaz
Red and ripe, every one. Wash your hands before you rub your eyes, hero.

Naga Watch #298

Defeat 10 Oasis Nagas south of Ashen Oasis, then report to Hunt Warden Bijan on the open sand 3 blocks east of his north-west house in Ashen Oasis. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Oasis NagaTurn in to Hunt Warden Bijan
Ten nagas. The water carriers can walk south again.

Restless Dead #299

Defeat 10 Ashen Revenants south-east of Ashen Oasis, then report to Hunt Warden Bijan on the open sand 3 blocks east of his north-west house in Ashen Oasis. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Ashen RevenantTurn in to Hunt Warden Bijan
Ten fewer. May they stay down this time.

Basilisk Scales #300

Bring 3 Ashen Basilisk Scales to Hunt Warden Bijan on the open sand 3 blocks east of his north-west house in Ashen Oasis. The Ashen Basilisks north-east of Ashen Oasis drop them. A one-time job.

Goal Bring 3 Ashen Basilisk ScaleTurn in to Hunt Warden Bijan
Three scales, still warm. Roxana will want these tonight.

Bread for the Well #301

Take Cook Simin's flatbread and stew to Warden Darius in his hall on the east side of the plaza in Ashen Oasis.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Warden Darius
From Simin? Still warm. Tell her I'll eat it at my post, and thank her for me.

Ash and Dust #302

Bring 5 Ashen Dust to Ash Guard Rostam on the south-east road, by the tent at the edge of Ashen Oasis. The Ash Ghouls well to the north-east of Ashen Oasis leave it behind when they fall.

Goal Bring 5 Ashen DustTurn in to Ash Guard Rostam
Five handfuls. I'll scatter it on the wind, far from the well.

A Green Sprout #303

Take Little Roshan's green sprout to Priestess Anahita on her porch north of the plaza in Ashen Oasis.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Priestess Anahita
A green shoot, growing out of the ash? Tell Roshan I have seen it, and that it gives me hope. Tell him to keep watering it.
