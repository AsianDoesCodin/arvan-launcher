Shakokonai City quests

Lv 25-35. A big cherry-blossom city in the east, where mining is introduced. 6 quests.

Restless Graves #31

Defeat 8 Grave Knights on the old battlefield north-east of Shakokonai, then return to King Hayato. Their third swing is the heavy one: step back after two.

Goal Defeat 8 Grave KnightTurn in to King Hayato
The graves are quiet. The king has a harder task in mind.

The Warden's Grove #32

Defeat 4 Verdant Wardens in the overgrown woods east of the city, toward the coast. When one plants its feet and roots glow, get out of the circle.

Goal Defeat 4 Verdant WardenTurn in to King Hayato
The woods are ours again.

Iron Dread #33

Bring down the Dread Juggernaut that holds the river ruins south-west of the city, then report to King Hayato.

Goal Defeat 1 Dread JuggernautTurn in to King Hayato
The river ruins are the city's again. One shadow remains under the mountain.

The Lion Monk #34

Defeat the Lion Monk at the foot of the great mountain east of the palace and return to King Hayato.

Goal Defeat 1 Lion MonkTurn in to King HayatoReward 2 Honing Stone
Shakokonai honors you. Take these stones to the royal smiths and have your gear honed.

Palace Bounty #35

Defeat 12 Grave Knights and report to a Royal Guard at the palace gate. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Grave KnightTurn in to Royal Guard
The palace pays its bounties in full.

Breaking Ground #36

Mine 3 Copper Ore and 3 Iron Ore with the Hearth Starter Pickaxe Foreman Ishida gave you, then bring them to him. The pick marks on your map show the nearest seams.

Goal Bring 3 Copper Ore, 3 Iron OreTurn in to Foreman Ishida
Good, clean ore. You have the arm for it. Keep the pick; Morikawa buys whatever you dig up.
