Westmarch quests

Lv 60-80. A town of log towers in a pine forest. 15 quests.

The Road to Westmarch #102

King Aurelian has asked you to carry word to Westmarch, in the western woods about 850 blocks north of Rustcliff. Find Reeve Aldric there. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Reeve Aldric
King Aurelian sent you? Then you are welcome in Westmarch. A dead rider called the Bone Cavalier haunts the old road, every bandit in the west rides in his shadow, and the beasts of the woods run wild behind them.

Bandit Brawlers #103

Defeat 8 Bandit Brawlers in the western woods south-west of Westmarch, then return to Reeve Aldric in Westmarch.

Goal Defeat 8 Bandit BrawlerTurn in to Reeve Aldric
That's 8 fewer. The people of Westmarch can breathe a little.

Timber Lions #104

Defeat 8 Timber Lions in the western woods north-west of Westmarch, then return to Ranger Wren in Westmarch.

Goal Defeat 8 Timber LionTurn in to Ranger Wren
8 of them, and not a scratch on you? Westmarch is lucky you came.

Bark Centipedes #105

Defeat 8 Bark Centipedes in the western woods south-east of Westmarch, then return to Ranger Wren in Westmarch.

Goal Defeat 8 Bark CentipedeTurn in to Ranger Wren
Good work. The ground out there is ours again, for now.

Bandit Enforcers #106

Defeat 3 Bandit Enforcers in the western woods north-east of Westmarch, then return to Reeve Aldric in Westmarch.

Goal Defeat 3 Bandit EnforcerTurn in to Reeve Aldric
3 of those brutes. Even the old hunters never managed that. You're ready for what's next.

The Pine Serpent #107

Defeat the Pine Serpent in the western woods well to the west of Westmarch, then return to Reeve Aldric in Westmarch.

Goal Defeat 1 Pine SerpentTurn in to Reeve Aldric
It's down! I saw the smoke from here. Only one thing is left out there now, and you know what it is.

The Bone Cavalier #108

Defeat the Bone Cavalier in the western woods well to the south of Westmarch, then return to Reeve Aldric in Westmarch. Its shield turns most blows, so strike when it lowers it. Bring friends and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 Bone CavalierTurn in to Reeve AldricReward 2 Honing Stone
With the Bone Cavalier down, the bandits are scattering. The carts are rolling again. West of here, where the Thornwood meets the lagoon, the fishing village of Reedwater has stopped sending its boats. Elder Mirela could use a sword.
Next: The Road to Reedwater

Westmarch Bounty #109

Defeat 12 Bandit Brawlers in the western woods south-west of Westmarch and report to Ranger Wren. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Bandit BrawlerTurn in to Ranger Wren
Good hunting. Westmarch pays its hunters fairly.

Bloodthorn for the Brews #187

Pick 8 Bloodthorn Berries from the briar patches in the pine woods around Westmarch and bring them to Herbalist Rowan. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Bloodthorn BerriesTurn in to Herbalist Rowan
Ripe and whole. The rangers will be swinging like bears tonight.

Timber Lion Cull #188

Defeat 10 Timber Lions north-west of Westmarch, then report to Hunt Warden Corin in the ledger room of the south-west tower in Westmarch. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Timber LionTurn in to Hunt Warden Corin
Ten lions. Osgar can go back to his logs.

Centipede Nests #189

Defeat 10 Bark Centipedes south-east of Westmarch, then report to Hunt Warden Corin in the ledger room of the south-west tower in Westmarch. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Bark CentipedeTurn in to Hunt Warden Corin
Good riddance. The nests will be cold by morning.

Enforcer's Buckles #190

Bring 3 Enforcer's Buckles to Hunt Warden Corin in the ledger room of the south-west tower in Westmarch. The Bandit Enforcers north-east of Westmarch drop them. A one-time job.

Goal Bring 3 Enforcer's BuckleTurn in to Hunt Warden Corin
Three buckles. The Reeve will sleep better tonight, and you'll be richer.

Stew for the Watch #191

Take Innkeeper Marta's stew to Ranger Wren in the map room of the north-east house.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Ranger Wren
Marta's stew? Bless her. Tell her the watch owes her another one.

Proof of the Purge #192

Bring 5 Bandit Insignias to Gatewarden Bertil at the east gate on the main road in Westmarch. The Bandit Brawlers south-west of Westmarch drop them.

Goal Bring 5 Bandit InsigniaTurn in to Gatewarden Bertil
Five insignias. I'll nail them over the gate so the rest know what's waiting.

Pressed Flowers #193

Take Young Edda's pressed flowers to Reeve Aldric in the bookshelf hall east of the market.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Reeve Aldric
Edda's flowers? Ha. Tell her they'll hang right here over my desk. The girl's right, the place needed colour.
