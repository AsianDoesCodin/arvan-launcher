Pinecross quests

Lv 60-80. A pine-wood village haunted by the Wild Hunt. 15 quests.

The Road to Pinecross #142

Castellan Durgan has asked you to carry word to Pinecross, in the pine woods about 650 blocks west of Stonecrest. Find Mayor Edric there. Follow the waypoint.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Mayor Edric
Castellan Durgan sent you? Then you are welcome in Pinecross. A storm has sat over the pines for a month, and the Wild Hunt rides in it. Its hounds and riders take whatever strays from the road.

Pine Wolves #143

Defeat 8 Pine Wolves in the pine woods north of Pinecross, then return to Mayor Edric in Pinecross.

Goal Defeat 8 Pine WolfTurn in to Mayor Edric
That's 8 fewer. The people of Pinecross can breathe a little.

Gale Imps #144

Defeat 8 Gale Imps in the pine woods south-west of Pinecross, then return to Huntsman Orrin in Pinecross.

Goal Defeat 8 Gale ImpTurn in to Huntsman Orrin
8 of them, and not a scratch on you? Pinecross is lucky you came.

Bark Pangolins #145

Defeat 8 Bark Pangolins in the pine woods east of Pinecross, then return to Huntsman Orrin in Pinecross.

Goal Defeat 8 Bark PangolinTurn in to Huntsman Orrin
Good work. The ground out there is ours again, for now.

Hunt Riders #146

Defeat 3 Hunt Riders in the pine woods north-east of Pinecross, then return to Mayor Edric in Pinecross.

Goal Defeat 3 Hunt RiderTurn in to Mayor Edric
3 of those brutes. Even the old hunters never managed that. You're ready for what's next.

The Sky Serpent #147

Defeat the Sky Serpent in the pine woods well to the south of Pinecross, then return to Mayor Edric in Pinecross.

Goal Defeat 1 Sky SerpentTurn in to Mayor Edric
It's down! I saw the smoke from here. Only one thing is left out there now, and you know what it is.

The Storm Hunter #148

Defeat the Storm Hunter in the pine woods well to the south-east of Pinecross, then return to Mayor Edric in Pinecross. Bring friends and potions.

Goal Defeat 1 Storm HunterTurn in to Mayor EdricReward 2 Honing Stone, 1 Reroll Stone
The storm has broken and the sun is on the pines again. Pinecross will drink to you for a month. North-west, where the woods end at the desert, the castle of Redspire guards the road into the sand. Margrave Aldous has asked for help.
Next: The Road to Redspire

Pinecross Bounty #149

Defeat 12 Pine Wolves in the pine woods north of Pinecross and report to Huntsman Orrin. A one-time bounty.

Goal Defeat 12 Pine WolfTurn in to Huntsman Orrin
Good hunting. Pinecross pays its hunters fairly.

Millet for the Ovens #267

Pick 8 Golden Millet from the open fields around Pinecross and bring them to Herbalist Bettony at the herb stall in the east market. A one-time order.

Goal Bring 8 Golden MilletTurn in to Herbalist Bettony
Heavy heads, every one. Hetty's ovens will be full tonight.

Imps in the Wind #268

Defeat 10 Gale Imps south-west of Pinecross, then report to Hunt Warden Wulfric on the open grass square north of the fountain in Pinecross. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Gale ImpTurn in to Hunt Warden Wulfric
Ten imps. The roofers send their thanks, and so does my ceiling.

Bark and Scale #269

Defeat 10 Bark Pangolins east of Pinecross, then report to Hunt Warden Wulfric on the open grass square north of the fountain in Pinecross. A one-time job.

Goal Defeat 10 Bark PangolinTurn in to Hunt Warden Wulfric
Ten pangolins. The timber carts can roll again.

Riders' Seals #270

Bring 3 Hunt Rider's Seals to Hunt Warden Wulfric on the open grass square north of the fountain in Pinecross. The Hunt Riders north-east of Pinecross drop them. A one-time job.

Goal Bring 3 Hunt Rider's SealTurn in to Hunt Warden Wulfric
Three seals. The Mayor will hang them in the hall where everyone can see.

Supper for the Huntsman #271

Take Cook Hetty's pot of stew to Huntsman Orrin at the lodge on the north side of Pinecross.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Huntsman Orrin
Hetty's stew? Still hot, in this weather. Tell her the hunt owes her a haunch.

Wolf Fangs #272

Bring 5 Pine Wolf Fangs to Road Guard Aldwin on the east road at the edge of Pinecross. The Pine Wolves north of Pinecross drop them.

Goal Bring 5 Pine Wolf FangTurn in to Road Guard Aldwin
Five fangs. I'll string them on the road post; the pack will smell them and think twice.

A Horseshoe for the Smith #273

Take the giant horseshoe Little Robin found to Smith Ulla at the forge in the great hall in the middle of Pinecross.

Goal Gather itemsTurn in to Smith Ulla
Where did Robin find this? Cold iron, and no farm horse ever wore it. Tell Robin it's from a Hunt horse, sure enough, and to stay out of the woods.
