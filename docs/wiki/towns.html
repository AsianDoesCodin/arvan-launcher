Towns and regions

Arvan is a chain of towns, each stronger than the last. Every town has shops, a smith, market stalls and people walking the streets.

The route

  1. Valemont is where every adventurer begins. It is a safe starting area.
  2. The Outpost, reached through a portal, is an allied camp with supply quests.
  3. Redroof, Shakokonai City and Greywall Citadel carry the story to the high 30s.
  4. Akamori ends the main story, with a volcano and the Fire Temple to its north.
  5. Twelve journey villages follow, from level 60 to 80, then the world opens up.
