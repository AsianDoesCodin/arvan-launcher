Patch notes

What's new in Arvan

Every update to the live server, newest first. The launcher installs the new files for you; just relaunch.

October 31, 2026Latest

Master mode, Guild armour and stone engraving

New

  • Master mode for all four dungeons. Clear a dungeon's normal mode at S+ yourself to unlock its Master mode. Monsters hit much harder, and you get double Guild Seals, double seal progress on the RNG meter and double monster materials.
  • Nine Guild armour looks: Vanguard (Lv 80), Warden (Lv 100) and Champion (Lv 120), each in heavy, light and arcane. Guild pieces you already own switch to the new look.
  • Stone Engraver Corwin at the City fountain sockets stones into your gear, combines them and engraves gear. Stones are crafted from mining ores, so mining pays off.
  • Material Merchant stocks more high-level monster materials and has a new Trophies page.

Changes

  • Guild gear stats raised to 1.2× the crafted gear of the same level.
  • Buff companions now give stats instead of potion effects: armour and HP, mana regen and max mana, attack speed, move speed, mining fortune, extra herbs and more fishing. Every companion's description now says what its perk and stats do.
  • Rarity Upgrader opens its own page with your worn gear and the rarity ladder.
  • Dungeons are fully lit. Mounts can't be summoned inside, and your companion stays with you when you enter or leave.
  • Dungeon Warden Ulric lists each dungeon by its starting level (for example Lv 80+).

Fixes

  • Attack Damage no longer stops at 2,048.
  • Opening a dungeon's cracked wall now counts as finding the secret room for your grade, even if you don't walk in.
October 30, 2026

Signature weapons and companion perks

New

  • 24 Signature weapons: one per class for each dungeon, forged at Ulric's Weapon Forge tab.
  • Perfect Honing and Perfect Reroll Stones are new rare dungeon chest rewards, and the RNG meter can now aim for them. Room chests can hold Rarity Upgraders.
  • Companion perks: every companion has a perk (Hoarder, Medic, Guardian, Ember Bite, Quick Feet and more), plus a rarity and a level you raise.
  • New dungeon screens: a fresh chest and reward view with real item pictures, and a small pop-up for room chest loot.

Changes

  • Guild gear has its own aqua Guild rarity. Hone caps are +6, +9 and +12 by tier, and your honing carries over for free when you forge the next Guild tier.

Fixes

  • Dying in a dungeon no longer drops you outside before you respawn.
  • Dungeon props like bells, carpets and levers no longer end up in your bag.
October 29, 2026

Dungeons are open

New

  • Four dungeons through Dungeon Warden Ulric at the City fountain: Goblin Warren (Lv 80+), Orc Warlord's Hold (Lv 100+), Creepy Mansion (Lv 112+) and the Monster Pyramid (Lv 134+).
  • Every run builds a new layout of rooms. Clear the rooms to reach the boss, and get graded on how well you did.
  • Guild Seals come from paid dungeon chests. Take them to the Guild Blacksmith in the guildhall to forge Guild gear.
October 28, 2026

New hunting grounds

New

  • Orc Warlord's Hold, the Creepy Mansion and the Monster Pyramid are now in the world, full of new monsters.
  • More monsters on the Asura grounds, new guild jobs and requests, and new monster drops for crafting.
October 24, 2026

New gear tooltips

Changes

  • Gear tooltips have a new layout with stats, needs and rarity easy to read. Smith and shop previews use the same layout.
  • Duelist skills raised to full strength.

Fixes

  • Shop armour sets each have their own look again.
  • Fixed an error that removed equipped gear.
  • The smith's "you have" mark now counts only the exact piece you own.
October 23, 2026

Duelist rework

New

  • Duelist rebuilt with eight new skills: Quickdraw, Bloodpaint, Severing Edge, Lacerate, Deaden, Wolf Emblem, Moonlit Tempest and Executioner's Cut. Skill damage scales with weapon speed.
  • Crit chance now shows on gear tooltips.

Changes

  • Weapons have attribute needs.
  • Gear is class-locked: arcane gear is for Flame, Ice and Priest; heavy and light gear is for Paladin, Reaver and Duelist.
October 22, 2026

Class and gear overhaul

New

  • Six classes from Lv 1: Paladin, Reaver, Duelist, Flame, Ice and Priest, picked on a new class screen when you make your character. Every character started fresh.
  • Protected loot: monster drops belong to you and your party. Right-click a drop to pick it up. Item names show their rarity colour.
  • The Attributes page has +1, +5, +10 and All buttons, and the inventory screen has a Settings button.

Changes

  • Smith recipes and shop gear from Lv 20 to Lv 48 cost much less.
